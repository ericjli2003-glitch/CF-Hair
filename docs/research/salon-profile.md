## 11. Pain points and opportunities

1. **Invisible online.** No website, no confirmed socials, unclaimed Yelp, zero reviews. Any modern web presence (bilingual site, claimed Google/Yelp listings, review requests after each visit) is an immediate win.
2. **Phone-only booking (probable).** In a small salon the stylist is usually mid-cut when the phone rings, so calls go unanswered and clients book elsewhere. A 24/7 AI receptionist that answers calls and chats, books into a real calendar, and texts confirmations directly addresses this. Unverified as a complaint (no reviews exist), but it is the standard failure mode for phone-only salons.
3. **Multilingual clientele.** Mandarin/Cantonese (and Korean nearby) speakers are underserved by English-only booking tools. A booking assistant that speaks English, Mandarin, Cantonese and Korean is a strong differentiator; no competitor found offers it.
4. **Same-building competition.** At least four salons share Henderson Place Mall, and The SOOM already has Fresha online booking. CF needs online booking at minimum to stay at parity.
5. **Name confusion.** "The Studio by CF & K" in the same mall and an unrelated @cf_hair_studio Instagram make search results noisy. Consistent naming ("CF Hair Salon Coquitlam") and a claimed Google Business Profile matter.
